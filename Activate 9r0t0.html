Chapter 1: Power on.

Back

Carefully hanging the chain around her neck, Professor Stoltor inserted the charm into her chest.

Instantly, her blackened eyes lit up green, and she lifted her head to "look" at him, lenses automatically adjusting to find the clearest view.

"Run command: introducction," Professor Stoltor instructed. The android blinked once, then gave a short nod.

"Greetings. I am Prototype 9r0t0. I was built by the Professor Charlie Stoltor, as a perfect metal replica of the human body. While I exist mainly as a proof of concept, I am capable of full human flexibility, strength, and more. I am modeled like a child both for conservation of resources, and for demonstration of how compact my components can be. How may I assist?" 9r0t0- 'Proto'- answered dutifully.

Professor Stoltor nodded, satisfied. "Hello. I am your creator, Professor Charlie Stoltor. List your primary functions."

"I can perform almost any task asked of me, if shown how to do it once. My number one directive is to obey instruction from humans, prioritizing instruction from my maker," it replied, "I am still early in development and have much to learn. I may ask questions I do not understand the significance of in order to expand my knowledge. I thank you for your patience."

"Do you have your base knowledge folder at hand?"

"yes sir."

"Good. Follow me upstairs to the kitchen and make a cup of tea," Professor Stoltor instructed, nodding. A simple task, yes, but adequate to test motor skills and command processing. 9r0t0 scanned the room, then looked down at the floor from where she sat on the table.

"How do I get down?" She asked, tilting her head to the side.

Offer to help her down, or let it figure this out alone.
