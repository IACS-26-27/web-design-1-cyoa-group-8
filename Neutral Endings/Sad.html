Chapter 2: Death is failure.

Back

"Death is the end," Professor Stoltor explained solemnly, "Death is being broken beyond repair, and becoming inable to function or fufil tasks. Death is considered inevitable for living things. It is the tragedy that stains all of history."

"Will I die?" 9r0t0 asked, looking up at him with large green eyes that glinted slightly as the cameras behind them adjusted to watch his face.

"No, you will not. At least, not like organic things do. You may need new parts or updates over time, but you cannot die."

"Understood."

"Good. Now, let's go to the kitchen and make that tea." Professor Stoltor instructed. 9r0t0 nodded and turned away from the dying crocus, following its creator up the stairs.

Its movements were clumsy at first; a poor immitation of how easily its maker moved. But soon enough, 9r0t0 realized the discrepancy lay in the difference in height, and adjusted acordingly. Similarly when the two reached the stairs, 9r0t0 paused momentarily to study the way the Professor moved up them. But this time, it produced a better result much faster, and each step it took was cleaner than the last.
