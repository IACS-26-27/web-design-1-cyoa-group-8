Chapter 3: Making Tea

Back

Upon reaching the kitchen, Professor Stoltor stepped forward and began digging through his cabinets, searching for his tea bags, kettle, and mugs. Everything was so disorganized... and reaching up was getting hard these days. He ought to have given the bot longer limbs, just to spare himself the ache. Still, he managed to get everything together after a moment and laid the materials out on the counter before turning back to 9r0t0, who was watching closely.

"Right. 9r0t0, look in your 'common knowledge' folder, and based on the videos there, deliver a cup of tea to me," The Professor instructed, stepping back to go sit in his favorite armchair by the heater and observe. 9r0t0 nodded, then turned back to the counter and scanned the available materials. It stool still for a moment, processing. Then it picked up the kettle and took it to the sink to fill with water.
