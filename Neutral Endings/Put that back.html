Chapter 2: Don't touch that, 9r0t0.

Back

"Put that back," Stoltor sighed. Immediately, 9r0t0 set the plant back on the shelf. Still... it did not take its camera eyes off of the small sprout.

"What kind of plant is it?" 9r0t0 asked.

"Hm? Oh, I don't know. Crocus, I believe. Does it matter?"

"I do not know. I am collecting data." it answered, slowly turning back to stare up at its creator once more. "Why is it here?"

He hesitated. "...It was a gift."

"It is slightly yellow in some places," 9r0t0 noted, still staring at him.

"Yes. It doesn't get enough light down here."

"Is it dying?"

"Not all at once, as far as I'm aware. It could probably still be fixed up, but I've got more important matters to concern myself with."

9r0t0 was quiet for a while. The Professer let it be quiet. It was obviously processing this, filing information away in its databanks for later reference. He raised one eyebrow when it turned its face to stare at the plant again.

"What is death?" the metal child asked simply.

  • "Becoming broken beyond repair."
