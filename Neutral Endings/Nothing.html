Chapter 2: Death is simply the absence of life.

Back

"Death is just another state of being," The Professor answered, "The opposite of living. Dead is not alive. Alive is functional, to put it simply. Breathing, thinking, moving."

"Am I alive?" 9r0t0 asked, tilting its head to the side, "I breathe with pumps and move with electricity. I think with coede."

"...No. You are not. At least, not the way I am. And not the way the plant is, either. Living things are organic."

"Understood," it nodded.

Silence stretched on. Neither maker nor machine broke it. Eventually, Professor Stoltor gestured to the staris, and the two climbed slowly. He only needed to go up two or three before 9r0t0 began to mimic his movements and climb up after him, adjusting as it went to better keep balance and match his pace with smaller legs.
