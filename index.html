Chapter 1: The beginning.

Twelve years. Twelve years of anger, of pain, of loss. Twelve years of work. Of creation. Of progress.

And now she was finally done.

Professor Charlie Stoltor stepped back, carefully removing thick leather gloves covered in motor oil. The last test runs hadn't been completely disasterous, and with these small attunements, things ought to go much better this time.

On the work bench sat a small girl, baby blue dress poofing out around her. Inside her rested many things she herself had no need for, but proved his methods without even the shadow of a doubt. Metal limbs like prosthetics; metal ribs to support a metal chest. Strong pumps for lungs and a heart. A thick woven membrane filled with stones and acids for a stomach, and much, much more. The only thing she didn't have was a brain. Instead, she had a compact super computer with a code of his own design; which Charlie argued she was probably better off with. Brains came with emotions, and those weren't worth programing.

Now all she needed was activation. Professor Stoltor lifted the golden locket, triple checking the activator mechanism one last time.
